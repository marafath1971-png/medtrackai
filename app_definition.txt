========================================================================
MEDAI — APP DEFINITION AND TARGET AUDIENCE
========================================================================

1. WHAT IS MEDAI?
-----------------
Medai is a premium, consumer-facing AI-powered Medication Management & 
Habit-building application (inspired by premium consumer apps like Medisafe, 
Cal AI, and Duolingo). It turns the daily routine of tracking medications 
and supplements into a sleek, engaging, and personalized habit loop.

2. CORE FEATURES & VALUE PROPOSITIONS
-------------------------------------
* AI-Powered Scanner Hub:
  - Pill Identifier: Computer vision identifies pills from camera scans.
  - Supplement Interaction Scanner: Scans and flags risky interactions 
    between medications and vitamins/supplements.
  - AI Quick-Log: Uses natural language processing to log doses or query
    schedule details.
* Gamification & Motivation:
  - Streaks & Milestones: Employs streak metrics (e.g. 3-day shield, 100-day 
    crown) and smooth haptic feedback to reward regular adherence.
  - Med Wrapped: A Spotify Wrapped/Duolingo-style visual summary of adherence 
    data to drive social sharing and virality.
* Caregiver & Multi-Profile Support:
  - Secure profile switching with PIN protection so users can manage schedules 
    for dependents (children, elderly relatives) safely.
* Smart & Style-Rich Notifications:
  - Personalized alerts ranging from gentle nudges to firm reminders, complete
    with health tips or motivational messaging.
* Premium Experience:
  - High-end dark mode aesthetics, smooth micro-animations, custom low-stock 
    alerts, and visualizer modules matching Apple Human Interface standards.

3. TARGET AUDIENCE DEFINITION
-----------------------------
* DEMOGRAPHICS:
  - Age:
    - Core Group (30–55): Professionals managing daily stress, mental health,
      prescriptions, or early chronic conditions (e.g. hypertension).
    - Elderly & Caregivers (55+): Multi-medication users requiring help 
      with complex schedules.
  - Tech Literacy: Moderate to high. They expect beautiful, responsive, 
    low-friction interactions (e.g. scanning a label over manual entry).
  - Income Level: Middle-to-upper class (target audience for premium digital 
    health subscription models).

* PSYCHOGRAPHICS:
  - The "Habit Builder": Wellness-focused users tracking vitamins, supplements, 
    and lifestyle health. Driven by streaks and progress visuals.
  - The "Anxious Manager": Users with conditions like Diabetes, Heart Disease, 
    or Anxiety who need peace of mind and strict schedule compliance.
  - The "Dedicated Caregiver": Users managing complex medication schedules 
    for spouses, parents, or children.

* PAIN POINTS SOLVED:
  - Forgetting doses due to busy schedules.
  - Running out of medication (low-stock warning card).
  - Lack of compiled adherence logs for doctor consultations.
  - Confusion over complex schedule setups.
